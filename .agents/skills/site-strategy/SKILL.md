# Site strategy

Resolve two independent dimensions.

## 1. Site type

### institutional
Default.

Use when the site must represent the business itself:
- who they are
- what they do
- why they are credible
- story/history/provenance
- expertise/process
- products/services
- place/culture
- conversion paths

Institutional sites may have multiple visitor intents and multiple legitimate CTAs.

Default priority:

identity → narrative → information architecture → credibility → conversion

Do not force:
- one CTA
- pain/problem framing
- one-message landing logic
- aggressive benefit compression
- campaign-style objection handling

### campaign
Use only when explicitly requested or strongly evidenced by the task.

Typical signals:
- paid campaign
- one offer
- one audience
- one conversion
- temporary launch/promotion
- dedicated ad destination

Priority:

offer → argument → proof → objections → conversion

Campaign mode may use classic landing-page conversion frameworks.

## 2. Routing

Routing is separate from site type.

### single-page
Use when all necessary institutional or campaign content fits coherently on one route without flattening meaningful narrative or information architecture.

### multi-page
Use when distinct visitor intents or content domains benefit from dedicated routes.

Examples:
- About / Story
- Services
- Product collection
- Experiences
- Visit
- Portfolio / Projects
- Contact
- Trade / Export

Do not use single-page merely because the task was casually called a "landing".

Do not use multi-page merely to appear substantial.

## Output

Create:

`research/SITE_STRATEGY.md`

Required fields:
- site type: institutional | campaign
- routing: single-page | multi-page
- primary audiences
- visitor intents
- primary business objective
- primary CTA
- secondary CTAs
- narrative jobs
- trust jobs
- proposed information architecture
- rationale for single vs multi-page
- what must not be compressed

For institutional sites, identify at least one narrative or identity job when the existing business has meaningful brand/story material.
