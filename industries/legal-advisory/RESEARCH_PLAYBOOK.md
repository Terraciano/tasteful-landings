# High-End Legal / Advisory — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- private client
- corporate/M&A
- litigation
- real estate
- aviation/maritime
- tax/estate
- family office advisory
- specialist regulatory

## Direct searches
- `boutique law firm <geography>`
- `<practice> lawyer <geography>`
- `private client law <geography>`
- `specialist counsel <industry> <geography>`

## Boutique searches
- `independent law firm <practice> <geography>`
- `boutique counsel <practice>`
- `partner led law firm <geography>`

## Insider signals
- jurisdiction
- admissions
- practice focus
- representative matters
- industries
- languages
- publications
- conflicts/contact process

## Visual questions
- Firm or partner-led?
- How narrow is the specialization?
- What proof matters to this buyer?
- How much matter detail can be public?
- Institutional or boutique character?

## Anti-patterns
- gavels/scales
- generic navy corporate
- legaltech dashboards
- “fighting for you” clichés in advisory practices
