# Boutique Hospitality / Villas / Resorts — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for boutique hotels, villas, lodges, estates, rural hospitality, and destination stays.

The site sells place, experience, confidence, and booking intent.

## Audience
- leisure travelers
- couples/families
- event guests
- destination travelers
- luxury travel advisors

## Conversions
- Check availability
- Book your stay
- Enquire privately
- Plan an experience
- Reserve a table/experience

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- sense of place
- architecture
- hospitality
- calm confidence
- specificity

Avoid:
- OTA-style commoditization
- generic travel stock
- app-like booking-first design
- luxury clichés

## Insider signals
- room/villa type
- occupancy
- location
- amenities
- inclusions
- dining
- experiences
- transfer/access
- policies where relevant

## SEO
- boutique hotel <location>
- luxury villa <location>
- lodge <location>
- vineyard hotel <location>
- boutique resort <location>
