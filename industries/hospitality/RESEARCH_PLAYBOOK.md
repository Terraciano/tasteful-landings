# Boutique Hospitality — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- boutique hotel
- villa
- lodge
- resort
- rural estate
- branded residence stay
- culinary/wine hospitality

## Direct searches
- `boutique hotel <geography>`
- `luxury villa <geography>`
- `lodge <geography>`
- `design hotel <geography>`
- `estate stay <geography>`

## Category leaders
Use relevant high-end hospitality brands plus local independents.

## Boutique searches
- `independent hotel <geography>`
- `design-led hotel <geography>`
- `private villa <geography>`
- `small luxury hotel <geography>`

## Insider signals
- room/villa categories
- occupancy
- check-in/out
- dining
- experiences
- transport/access
- seasonality
- cancellation/deposit
- concierge

## Visual questions
- Place, room, or experience as hero?
- Direct booking or enquiry?
- How much destination education is needed?
- Is architecture a differentiator?
- What does the guest need before committing?

## Anti-patterns
- OTA grid look
- generic tropical/European stock
- overlong cinematic intros
- hidden booking CTA
- fake “exclusive escape” language
