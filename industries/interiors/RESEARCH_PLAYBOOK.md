# High-End Interiors — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- residential interiors
- hospitality interiors
- interior architecture
- decorator
- furniture/design studio
- design-build interiors

## Direct searches
- `interior designer <geography>`
- `luxury interior design <geography>`
- `residential interior architect <geography>`

## Boutique searches
- `boutique interior studio <geography>`
- `independent interior designer <geography>`
- `residential design studio <geography>`

## Insider signals
- scope
- materials
- custom work
- sourcing
- architecture collaboration
- project type
- completion
- photographer

## Visual questions
- Project work or designer personality first?
- How much project text is necessary?
- Is the brand minimal, layered, classical, contemporary, or eclectic?
- What makes the work specific beyond “luxury”?
- Does the site need project taxonomy or a more editorial narrative?

## Anti-patterns
- beige-only moodboard sites
- Masonry/Pinterest as entire IA
- generic serif templates
- over-animated galleries
- empty “timeless luxury” copy
