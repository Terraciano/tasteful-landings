# High-End Interiors — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for interior designers, decorators, interior architects, furniture studios, and residential design firms. The site sells taste, authorship, fit, material judgment, and built proof.

## Audience
- homeowners
- developers
- architects
- hospitality clients
- owner representatives

## Primary conversions
- Discuss a project
- Request a consultation
- View selected work
- Enquire with the studio

## Brand characteristics
Prefer authorship, materiality, restraint, editorial confidence, strong photography, and project specificity.

Avoid Pinterest-template grids, SaaS layouts, generic beige luxury, decorative serif clichés, and over-animated galleries.

## Composition
Prefer project-led editorial layouts, full-bleed photography, asymmetry, material/detail shots, concise project metadata, and strong rhythm between image and text.

## Insider signals
- project location
- project type
- scope
- architect collaboration
- materials
- custom millwork/furnishings
- completion year
- photographer credit
- procurement/sourcing approach where relevant

## Copy tone
Specific, design-literate, restrained.

Avoid empty language such as “timeless elegance”, “elevated living”, and “bespoke luxury” unless the work actually supports it.

## SEO
- interior designer <location>
- luxury interior design <location>
- residential interior designer <location>
- interior architect <location>
