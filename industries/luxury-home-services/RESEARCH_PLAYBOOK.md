# Luxury Home Services — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory first
Examples:
- landscape design/build
- pools
- home automation
- millwork
- kitchens
- stone
- lighting
- estate maintenance
- specialty HVAC/environmental control

Do not research “luxury home services” as one generic category.

## Direct searches
- `<service> <geography>`
- `custom <service> <geography>`
- `high end <service> <geography>`
- `<service> architect partner <geography>`

## Boutique searches
- `specialist <service> <geography>`
- `bespoke <service> <geography>`
- `artisan <service> <geography>`
- `design build <service> <geography>`

## Insider signals
Define per subcategory:
- materials
- brands/systems
- process
- installation
- tolerances/technical requirements
- service/maintenance
- certifications
- project scale
- design collaboration

## Visual questions
- Is proof primarily finished work, process, or technical expertise?
- Is the buyer a homeowner or design professional?
- Does the service need architectural or technical language?
- Which details signal workmanship to insiders?
- What should feel premium without becoming “luxury branded”?

## Anti-patterns
- generic contractor websites
- badge walls
- SaaS/service cards
- stock tradespeople
- black/gold
- overclaiming “white glove” without operational proof
