# Luxury Home Services — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for premium landscaping, pools, home automation, specialty maintenance, bespoke kitchens, stonework, millwork, and other high-value residential service firms.

The site sells reliability, workmanship, taste, process, and trust inside expensive homes.

## Audience
- homeowners
- architects
- interior designers
- builders
- property managers
- estate managers

## Primary conversions
- Discuss a project
- Request a site visit
- Request a consultation
- View selected work
- Speak with the team

## Brand characteristics
Prefer craft, precision, professionalism, discretion, and material/process expertise.

Avoid contractor-template clichés, “smart home tech” futurism unless actually relevant, badge-heavy trust sections, and generic luxury styling.

## Insider signals
Depend on subcategory, for example:
- materials
- systems/vendors
- installation method
- service area
- maintenance model
- lead times
- trade partnerships
- certifications
- warranty/service process

- <service> <location>
- luxury <service> <location>
- custom <service> <location>
- high end <service> <location>
