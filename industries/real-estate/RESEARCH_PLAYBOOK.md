# Luxury Real Estate — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- residential brokerage
- developer/new development sales
- branded residences
- luxury rentals
- estate advisory
- relocation/international buyer
- off-market/private listings

## Direct searches
- `luxury real estate <geography>`
- `luxury realtor <geography>`
- `new development sales <geography>`
- `branded residences <geography>`
- `estate agent <neighborhood>`

## Category leaders
Inspect only relevant examples:
- Sotheby’s International Realty
- Christie's International Real Estate
- Douglas Elliman
- The Agency
- Compass Luxury

## Boutique/niche searches
- `boutique real estate <geography>`
- `independent luxury realtor <geography>`
- `private property advisor <geography>`
- `architectural homes <geography>`

## Insider signals
- neighborhood micro-market
- architect/developer
- lot/floor area
- fees/HOA
- completion date
- property tax context
- residency/investment context if relevant
- off-market mechanics

## Visual questions
- Is the agent/advisor or property the hero?
- Is inventory broad or curated?
- How important is neighborhood context?
- Is this architecture-led, lifestyle-led, or investment-led?
- How much pricing/spec detail should be visible?
- What signals discretion without feeling empty?

## Anti-patterns
- proptech dashboard look
- generic card grids
- fake “exclusive” claims
- stock skyline overload
- black/gold
- Miami-style neon luxury applied everywhere
