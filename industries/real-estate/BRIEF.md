# Luxury Real Estate — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for luxury residential brokerage, developer sales, branded residences, estates, and high-end rentals when explicitly relevant.

The site sells trust, access, local expertise, property quality, and deal confidence. It should not feel like a generic listing portal unless inventory is truly the core product.

## Audience
- buyers
- sellers
- investors
- family offices
- relocation buyers
- international buyers
- developers

## Primary conversions
- Request details
- Arrange a private viewing
- Speak with an advisor
- List a property
- Request an appraisal
- Enquire about availability

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- discretion
- local fluency
- editorial confidence
- architectural literacy
- relationship-led service
- selective inventory

Avoid:
- proptech/SaaS aesthetics
- “dream home” clichés
- fake scarcity
- black/gold
- endless glossy cards

## Composition
Prefer:
- immersive property photography
- editorial layouts
- map/geography where useful
- property details with restrained metadata
- advisor identity
- neighborhood/context storytelling

Avoid:
- portal-like grid dominance
- rounded listing-card overload
- dashboard filters unless needed
- generic “luxury lifestyle” stock

## Typography
Use typography with editorial or architectural character. Avoid automatic serif + gold luxury coding.

## Information architecture
1. precise hero
2. selected properties / developments
3. local expertise / geography
4. advisory / selling proposition
5. process / trust
6. advisor/team
7. inquiry

## Insider signals
- address / neighborhood
- architecture
- lot / floor area
- bedrooms / baths
- amenities
- HOA/condo context where relevant
- developer / architect
- completion status
- price guidance where publishable
- off-market status only if factual

## Copy tone
Specific, local, calm, confident.

Avoid:
- unparalleled living
- redefine luxury
- exclusive lifestyle
- elevate your life

## SEO
Intent families:
- luxury real estate <location>
- homes for sale <location>
- <neighborhood> real estate
- luxury realtor <location>
- new development <location>
- branded residences <location>

## Trust
Use only factual:
- licenses
- transaction history
- press
- developer relationships
- years
- offices
- local market coverage
