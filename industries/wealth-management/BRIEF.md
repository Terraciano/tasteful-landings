# Wealth Management / Family Office — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for wealth managers, family offices, private investment/advisory firms, and multi-family offices.

The site sells trust, discretion, alignment, clarity, and institutional competence.

## Audience
- HNW/UHNW families
- founders
- executives
- inheritors
- trustees/advisors

## Conversions
- Speak with an advisor
- Request a private conversation
- Contact the firm
- Discuss your situation

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- discretion
- continuity
- institutional calm
- stewardship
- clear thinking

Avoid:
- fintech UI
- dashboards/charts as decoration
- loud growth claims
- black/gold clichés

## Insider signals
- advisory model
- custody structure
- fiduciary status where applicable
- client type
- investment philosophy
- governance
- estate/tax coordination
- geographic scope

## SEO
Often secondary to referral/direct traffic, but useful intents include:
- wealth management <location>
- family office <location>
- private wealth advisor <location>
