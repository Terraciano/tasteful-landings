# Wealth Management / Family Office — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- RIA/wealth manager
- single family office
- multi-family office
- investment office
- private bank-adjacent advisory
- estate/governance advisory

## Direct searches
- `wealth management <geography>`
- `family office <geography>`
- `private wealth advisor <geography>`
- `multi family office <geography>`

## Category leaders
Inspect respected private wealth/family-office firms relevant to the jurisdiction.

## Boutique searches
- `independent wealth advisor <geography>`
- `boutique family office <geography>`
- `founder wealth advisor <geography>`

## Insider signals
- fiduciary/custody
- client minimums if public
- governance
- tax/estate coordination
- investment philosophy
- liquidity events
- reporting
- intergenerational planning

## Visual questions
- Firm or principal as hero?
- How much philosophy vs services?
- Is the audience institutional or family-led?
- What signals discretion without emptiness?
- How much regulatory disclosure is needed?

## Anti-patterns
- fintech dashboards
- stock-market charts
- generic city skylines
- “grow your wealth” hype
- black/gold private-bank cosplay
