# Olive Estates — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- estate producer
- premium consumer brand
- mill
- tasting/tourism
- export producer
- hospitality/farm shop

## Direct searches
- `olive oil estate <geography>`
- `extra virgin olive oil producer <geography>`
- `olive oil tasting <geography>`
- `olive mill <geography>`
- `premium olive oil <geography>`

## Category leaders
Use relevant high-quality producers from the target region plus international estates only when they solve a specific visual/commercial question.

## Boutique searches
- `single estate olive oil <geography>`
- `boutique olive oil producer <geography>`
- `olive farm tasting <geography>`

## Insider signals
- cultivar
- harvest
- extraction
- mill
- filtering
- acidity/polyphenols when useful
- storage
- origin
- estate
- packaging

## Visual questions
- Product or estate first?
- Is tourism important?
- How much process detail educates the buyer?
- What visual cues are truly regional?
- Is gastronomy central to the brand?

## Anti-patterns
- generic Mediterranean stock
- Italian cosplay
- commodity ecommerce
- fake rustic textures
- black/gold packaging clichés
