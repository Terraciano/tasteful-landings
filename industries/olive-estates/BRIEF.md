# Olive Estates / Premium Olive Oil — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for olive estates, premium EVOO producers, tasting experiences, farm shops, hospitality-adjacent estates, and export-focused producers.

The site sells origin, harvest, cultivar, process, freshness, estate identity, and gastronomy.

## Audience
- consumers
- chefs/restaurants
- importers/distributors
- food tourism visitors
- gift buyers

## Primary conversions
- Explore oils
- Buy / enquire
- Book a tasting
- Visit the estate
- Request trade information
- Contact export

## Brand characteristics
Prefer agricultural specificity, provenance, process, gastronomy, materiality, and place.

Avoid fake Tuscan aesthetics for non-Italian producers, generic “Mediterranean luxury”, commodity ecommerce, and black/gold.

## Insider signals
- cultivar
- harvest date
- extraction method
- mill
- acidity/analysis where relevant
- origin
- estate/parcel
- awards only if factual
- packaging formats

- extra virgin olive oil <region>
- olive oil tasting <region>
- olive estate <region>
- premium olive oil <country>
