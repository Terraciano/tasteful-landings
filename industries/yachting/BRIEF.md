# Yachting / Yacht Brokerage — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for yacht brokerage, acquisition, charter, management, and shipyard/builder contexts.

The buyer expects technical fluency, discretion, inventory clarity, and strong media.

## Audience
- owners
- buyers
- charter clients
- family offices
- captains/managers
- brokers/advisors

## Primary conversions
- Request yacht details
- Arrange a viewing
- Speak with a broker
- List your yacht
- Enquire about charter
- Discuss acquisition

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- maritime expertise
- understated confidence
- technical detail
- discretion
- global reach

Avoid:
- nightclub wealth signaling
- champagne-heavy stock
- generic Mediterranean glamour
- SaaS/app aesthetics

## Composition
Prefer:
- vessel-first imagery
- long horizontal compositions
- technical specifications
- deck/interior sequencing
- destination content only when commercially relevant

## Insider signals
- builder
- year/refit
- LOA
- beam/draft
- gross tonnage
- engines
- cruising/max speed
- range
- cabins/guests/crew
- classification/status
- asking price only if publishable

## Copy
Factual, concise, broker-like.

## SEO
- yachts for sale
- superyacht broker
- yacht charter <location>
- <builder/model> for sale
- yacht management
