# Yachting / Yacht Brokerage — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- brokerage/sales
- charter
- management
- new build
- shipyard/builder
- refit
- marina/service

## Direct searches
- `yacht broker <geography>`
- `superyacht brokerage <geography>`
- `yacht charter <geography>`
- `yacht management <geography>`
- `<builder> yacht for sale`

## Category leaders
- Burgess
- Fraser
- Northrop & Johnson
- Camper & Nicholsons
- Edmiston
- Y.CO

## Boutique searches
- `independent yacht broker`
- `boutique yacht brokerage`
- `private yacht advisor`
- `principal led yacht brokerage`

## Insider signals
- builder/year/refit
- LOA/beam/draft
- GT
- engines/speed/range
- cabins/crew
- classification
- VAT/status
- berth/location
- charter rate

## Visual questions
- Vessel or advisor as hero?
- Sales or charter primary?
- How technical should spec density be?
- Is destination imagery useful or distracting?
- What makes the brand maritime-specific rather than generic luxury?

## Anti-patterns
- generic champagne/yacht lifestyle
- black/gold
- glossy cards
- app-like inventory
- luxury travel clichés overpowering vessel detail
