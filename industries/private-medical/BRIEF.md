# Private Medical / Cosmetic / Longevity — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for private clinics, elective/cosmetic practices, dermatology, dentistry, longevity, and concierge medicine.

The site must balance trust, clinical legitimacy, discretion, and premium service.

## Audience
- private-pay patients
- international patients
- executives/families
- cosmetic patients
- referring professionals where relevant

## Conversions
- Book consultation
- Request appointment
- Speak with patient coordinator
- Enquire about treatment
- Request assessment

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- clinical calm
- physician credibility
- discretion
- clarity
- evidence
- hospitality-level service

Avoid:
- wellness-tech dashboard look
- miracle claims
- aggressive before/after marketing
- fake scientific visuals
- black/gold clinic luxury

## Insider signals
- physician credentials
- board certification
- specialty
- treatment scope
- consultation process
- facilities
- regulatory/licensing info
- outcomes only with proper evidence

- private clinic <location>
- cosmetic surgeon <location>
