# Private Medical / Cosmetic / Longevity — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- cosmetic/plastic surgery
- dermatology
- dentistry
- concierge medicine
- longevity
- diagnostics
- fertility
- private general medicine
- specialist clinic

## Direct searches
- `private clinic <geography>`
- `<specialty> clinic <geography>`
- `cosmetic surgeon <geography>`
- `concierge medicine <geography>`
- `longevity clinic <geography>`

## Category leaders
Use respected clinics/health systems relevant to the specialty and geography.

## Boutique searches
- `private <specialty> clinic <geography>`
- `boutique medical practice <geography>`
- `concierge <specialty> <geography>`

## Insider signals
- physician credentials
- specialty/subspecialty
- accreditation
- consultation flow
- diagnostics/equipment
- treatment indications
- recovery/logistics
- international patient support

## Visual questions
- Physician or facility as hero?
- Clinical or hospitality-led?
- How much treatment detail belongs on homepage?
- What trust proof is legally/ethically appropriate?
- How can premium service be signaled without weakening medical credibility?

## Anti-patterns
- fake science imagery
- biotech/SaaS UI
- miracle/outcome claims
- excessive before/after
- spa aesthetic for serious medicine
- unsupported superlatives
