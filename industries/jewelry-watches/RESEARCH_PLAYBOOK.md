# Fine Jewelry / Watches — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- independent jeweler
- bespoke atelier
- vintage/estate jewelry
- watch dealer
- vintage watches
- auction/private sales
- repairs/restoration

## Direct searches
- `fine jewelry <geography>`
- `bespoke jeweler <geography>`
- `vintage watches <geography>`
- `estate jewelry <geography>`
- `watch dealer <geography>`

## Category leaders
Inspect only relevant maisons, respected dealers, auction houses, and independent ateliers.

## Boutique searches
- `independent jeweler <geography>`
- `watch specialist <geography>`
- `vintage watch dealer <geography>`
- `bespoke jewelry atelier <geography>`

## Insider signals
- reference
- serial/year
- provenance
- condition
- service
- box/papers
- gemstone certificates
- materials
- maker marks
- restoration

## Visual questions
- Product or house story first?
- How macro/detail-heavy should imagery be?
- Direct purchase or private enquiry?
- How should provenance be surfaced?
- Is rarity or craftsmanship the main value?

## Anti-patterns
- black/gold
- generic Shopify luxury
- fake heritage
- velvet/spotlight stock overload
- app-like filters dominating the experience
