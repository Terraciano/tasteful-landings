# Fine Jewelry / Watches — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for independent jewelers, watch dealers, ateliers, estate jewelry, bespoke houses, and collector specialists.

The site sells object quality, provenance, craft, rarity, and trust.

## Audience
- collectors
- private clients
- gift buyers
- sellers/consignors
- trade buyers

## Primary conversions
- Enquire about a piece
- Request a private appointment
- Discuss bespoke work
- Sell / consign
- View the collection

## Brand characteristics
Prefer object-first presentation, material detail, craft, provenance, and quiet confidence.

Avoid black/gold by default, generic Shopify luxury, fake heritage, and glossy “wealth” clichés.

## Insider signals
- maker/brand
- reference
- year/period
- materials
- dimensions
- gemstones
- certificates
- provenance
- condition
- service history
- box/papers
- price if publishable
