# Collector / Exotic Automotive — Industry Brief

## Commercial context
Use for collector car dealers, exotic brokers, marque specialists, restoration shops, concours/service businesses, and private sales advisors.

The site sells inventory quality, provenance, specialist knowledge, and trust.

## Audience
- collectors
- enthusiasts
- investors
- sellers
- international buyers

## Conversions
- Enquire about a vehicle
- Request history/details
- Arrange inspection
- Sell/consign a car
- Speak with a specialist

## Brand characteristics
Prefer:
- marque fluency
- provenance
- restraint
- mechanical detail
- editorial catalog quality

Avoid:
- dealership-sale graphics
- crypto/exotic-car flex culture
- neon tech look
- fake scarcity

## Insider signals
- chassis/VIN
- model/year
- mileage
- ownership history
- matching numbers
- restoration history
- specification/options
- service records
- provenance
- concours history
- asking price if publishable

## SEO
- <model> for sale
- collector car dealer <location>
- classic Ferrari/Porsche/Mercedes dealer
- exotic car broker <location>
