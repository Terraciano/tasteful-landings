# Collector / Exotic Automotive — Research Playbook

## Resolve subcategory
- collector dealer
- exotic dealer
- marque specialist
- broker/advisor
- restoration
- consignment
- private collection sales

## Direct searches
- `collector cars <geography>`
- `classic car dealer <geography>`
- `Ferrari specialist <geography>`
- `Porsche specialist <geography>`
- `exotic car broker <geography>`

## Category leaders
Inspect relevant specialists, auction houses, and marque-focused dealers.

Examples:
- RM Sotheby's
- Gooding & Company
- Broad Arrow
- Canepa
- DK Engineering

## Boutique searches
- `independent collector car dealer`
- `marque specialist <brand>`
- `private car sales advisor`
- `restoration specialist <brand>`

## Insider signals
- chassis/VIN
- matching numbers
- build sheet
- restoration
- provenance
- ownership
- concours
- service
- paint/interior specification

## Visual questions
- Inventory or specialist identity first?
- How much spec/provenance should be immediately visible?
- Is photography studio-clean, archival, or road-context?
- Does the site need auction/catalog energy or private-sales restraint?

## Anti-patterns
- dealership sale badges
- supercar influencer aesthetic
- neon gradients
- generic three-card inventory
- fake scarcity
